🧠 Mental Health in Tech — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)


📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes mental health survey responses from individuals working in the tech industry. The goal is to understand how workplace culture, demographics, company size, and remote work environments influence employee mental health and treatment-seeking behavior.
Through detailed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), the project identifies patterns, risk factors, and actionable workplace insights that can help support employee well-being in the tech sector.

🎯 Business Objective

To uncover key trends and insights regarding mental health awareness, workplace support, and treatment behavior within tech employees, enabling companies to:

Improve mental health programs and benefits
Reduce stigma around mental health in the workplace
Create better organizational policies for employee well-being

📈 Key Insights (Summary)

Many respondents indicated mental health challenges affecting work performance.
Larger companies tend to offer better mental health benefits.
Employees with prior family history were more likely to seek treatment.
Remote workers showed a notable difference in treatment-seeking behavior (details shown in analysis).

💡 Recommendations

Increase mental health support programs, especially in small or mid-size companies.

Normalize mental health conversations within teams

Improve access to therapy, wellness resources, and anonymous support channels
